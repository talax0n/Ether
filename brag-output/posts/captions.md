## 1. Carousel: How it works
`how-1.png` → `how-6.png`

> Finding a hackathon team shouldn't mean posting "anyone need a designer?" in every group chat you're in.
> Pick your competition, swipe on solo students and teams, and when both sides say yes, the team forms and the contact links unlock.
> Scan the QR on the last slide to get the APK.

## 4. Single: How you earn XP
`xp.png`

> XP in Ether comes from outcomes, not swipes: completing your profile, forming a team, finishing a competition, getting endorsed by teammates, and having a teammate confirm your result.

> 8 levels, from Rookie (0 XP) to Legend (1,500 XP). Your level shows on your card, so teams can see you actually finish things.
