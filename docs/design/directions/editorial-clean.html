Editorial CleanDIRECTION 3 OF 3

A campus magazine that happens to keep score. Light paper, serif headlines, generous whitespace. Game mechanics are present but typeset, not shouted. One electric blue does all the "you did something" work.

Palette
Paper #FAFAF7 · White #FFFFFF (cards) · Hairline #E4E2DA · Ink #121212 · Graphite #55554F (secondary) · Electric #3B3BFF (like, XP, progress) · Signal #FF4F1F (new / alerts, tint #FFE6DD) · Lime #D4F55B (highlighter, only behind Ink text).
Type
Fraunces (variable serif, opsz 9–144) for names, numbers and headlines; italic for monograms and quotes. Inter 400–600 for UI. Scale: 64 level numeral / 54 match / 36 name / 23 list title / 15 body / 11 eyebrow caps +1.6 tracking. Tabular numerals on timers.
Shape
Radii 10 / 16 / 28, full pills for buttons and tags. 1dp hairlines do the structure: ledgers, fact rows, list dividers. One soft long shadow (0 24 48 −28, 35% ink) for the top card only. Circles for people and badges.
Motion
Quiet and exact. tween(400, FastOutSlowIn) for content; spring(dampingRatio 0.7, stiffness MediumLow) for the card and badge unlock (small overshoot, no wobble). Stagger 90 ms. Match = circles slide into a Venn → ink outline draws (600 ms) → 14 small paper bits → ledger lines fade up.
Iconography
Hairline 1.6 stroke icons (Material Symbols Outlined, weight 200). Mostly words instead of icons; icons only for nav and actions. Monograms in italic serif replace avatar placeholders.
Inspired by
Hinge profile layouts (prompt-as-quote), Strava Year in Sport (big numerals, editorial stats), Airbnb 2025 redesign (soft depth, restraint), Duolingo core tabs (clear hierarchy under the fun). See ../references.md [Editorial].
Compose build
Mostly plain Material 3 with a custom Typography (Fraunces via downloadable fonts) and HorizontalDivider hairlines. Venn = two Box(CircleShape) with BlendMode.Multiply in graphicsLayer. Outline draw = PathMeasure.getSegment animated. XP line = LinearProgressIndicator with custom track. Badge arc = drawArc.
Android risk
Lowest. No blur, no glow, few particles. Watch: variable-font axes (opsz) need API 26+ and a bundled font file; downloadable fonts fall back to the system serif on first launch, so bundle Fraunces in res/font. Multiply blend: if it bands on old GPUs, use a solid overlap color.
A11y
Ink on Paper 18:1, Graphite on Paper 7.3:1, White on Electric 6.9:1, Ink on Lime 15:1. Serif only ≥ 17sp; body stays Inter 14–16. Targets ≥ 48dp (Pass 64dp, Like 76dp). Reduced motion: content appears in place, no tilt loop, no bits.
