Arcade Direction 1 of 3

Chunky, outlined, tactile. Every surface looks pressable, every reward lands with a bounce. Duolingo energy for a campus crowd.

Palette
Ink #1E1B2E (outlines, text) · Cream #FFF7EA (bg) · Coral #FF5A36 (brand, kept from current app) · Sun #FFC233 (XP, energy) · Mint #2BD47D (like, success) · Sky #3BA7FF (events) · Grape #8B5CF6 (match, badges) · Muted #5B5670. Text is always Ink on bright fills (≥ 7:1). Never white on mint/sun.
Type
Nunito 700–900 (fallback system rounded). Scale: 52 display / 30 card name / 22 title / 17 body-bold / 15 body / 13 label caps +1 letter-spacing. Weight carries hierarchy; no light weights.
Shape
Radii 12 / 20 / 28, pills for chips. 3dp Ink outline on every container. Elevation = solid offset "ledge" (0 5dp 0 Ink), no blur shadows. Hex for level, circle medals for badges, dashed outline = empty slot.
Motion
Springy. Press: ledge collapses 5→1dp in 80 ms. Entrances: spring(dampingRatio 0.5, stiffness Medium) ≈ CSS cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1), 350–500 ms. Rewards stack: headline slam → avatars collide → heart pop → coins, 120 ms apart. Background rays rotate 18 s linear.
Iconography
Rounded 2.6 stroke icons (Material Symbols Rounded, weight 700, grade 200). Filled heart for like. Icons sit inside outlined "stickers" when they represent rewards.
Inspired by
Duolingo (3D ledge buttons, XP chest, streak flame), Duolingo level-up / league promo screens, Tinder stamp overlays, chunky "neo-brutalist" Dribbble shots. See ../references.md [Arcade].
Compose build
Ledge = two stacked Boxes or drawBehind offset rect; press via animateDpAsState. Card drag: Modifier.pointerInput + Animatable offset, rotation = offset/20. Rays = Canvas + rotate(). Confetti = Canvas particle list driven by withFrameNanos. XP bar = animateFloatAsState(spring) + count via animateIntAsState. Shine = Brush.linearGradient with animated offset.
Android risk
Low. No blur anywhere. Text stroke on headline: use drawText twice (stroke style then fill) or TextStyle(drawStyle = Stroke) (Compose 1.5+). Confetti capped at ~40 particles.
A11y
Body text Ink on Cream 15:1, Muted on Cream 6.4:1. Touch targets ≥ 48dp (action buttons 76/88dp). Swipe actions mirrored by buttons. Reduced motion: no rays, no confetti, no tilt loop; rewards appear in final state.
